# Workmates Code of Conduct

Workmates welcomes people who want to build, use, and improve the project. Everyone participating in its issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces is expected to help keep those spaces constructive.

## Participation

Be clear about the work and considerate toward the people doing it. Explain disagreements with evidence, accept corrections, and give others room to ask questions. Respect differences in experience, background, identity, and communication style.

The following conduct is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, or personal attacks.
- Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual content or attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption, targeted intimidation, or retaliation against someone who reports a concern.
- Impersonating another participant or misrepresenting project authority.

These expectations also apply when someone represents Workmates outside its own project spaces.

## Raising a concern

For conduct that needs moderator attention, contact the maintainers through an available contact method listed on the [Sidiora Labs GitHub profile](https://github.com/Sidiora-Labs). GitHub's reporting and blocking controls are also available for conduct on GitHub.

Describe what happened and include relevant links or dates where possible. Do not post another person's private information or sensitive evidence in a public issue. This repository does not designate a separate private moderation address or an independent reporting panel.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove content, ask participants to change their behavior, restrict participation, or ban an account from project spaces. The response should reflect the severity and persistence of the conduct and its effect on other participants.

Reports should be handled with discretion, sharing details only as needed to assess and respond. A maintainer who is the subject of a report should step back from deciding it when another uninvolved maintainer is available. Independent review cannot be promised when no such maintainer is available.

These expectations apply to maintainers as well as contributors. Raising a concern in good faith must not lead to retaliation.
